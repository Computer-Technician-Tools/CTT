## What is CTT?

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Computer Technician Tools (CTT)** is an open-source technical knowledge base created to make troubleshooting and maintaining technology easier.
>
> CTT collects useful information, tools, guides, references and practical solutions, making the right information to find quite a task, now you don't have to look for dozens of different websites every time something goes wrong.

## Story

This project began in **[2024](https://github.com/MRCYODev/Computer-Technician-Tools/commit/80c3686120d5106c7fda0aeb60f2ade1cfdcea9b)** with a simple goal: **Make it easier for me to troubleshoot and maintain my own computers, while building something useful enough to help other people do the same.**

> Since then, the idea has expanded beyond just computers and sought to cover as much technology as possible.

## 🤝 Contributions

CTT is open source, and contributions are welcome.

You can help by:

- Adding guides and useful tools.
- Fixing incorrect information.
- Adding troubleshooting procedures.
- Adding console and game modding information.
- Improving the website.
- Fixing bugs.
- Reporting outdated information.
- Improving the existing documentation.

Even a small contribution can help someone solve a problem.
