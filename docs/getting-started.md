# Get Started

## Welcome to Computer Technician Tools

>**CTT** is here help people learn more and new things about technology. Our goal is to make the greatest **Open Knowledge Archive** for people. **CTT** will always be free to use and open for everybody, you can use it to learn things and get help with troubleshooting your computers, gadgets, mobile devices and technology in general . we are always open to feedback and external help in order to expand our work even further, no matter how small or big of a change it might be. 

## Platforms we support

1. [Windows](/docs/platforms/operating-systems/windows/index.md)
2. [Linux](/docs/platforms/operating-systems/linux/index.md)
3. [MacOS](/docs/platforms/operating-systems/macos/index.md) (Coming Soon)
4. [Android](/docs/platforms/mobile/android/index.md)
5. [iOS](/docs/platforms/mobile/ios/index.md) (Coming Soon)

and much more

## CTT is currently under construction

:::warning
**CTT** is being built as a community-driven technical resource covering **repair, troubleshooting, diagnostics, emulation, and much more** across many types of technology.
:::

## Want to help?

**We'd love your contribution.**

You can help **CTT** by:

- Writing new Guides and Tutorials
- Adding Repair and Troubleshooting procedures
- Adding useful Tools and Software
- Documenting Console and Handheld Modifications
- Adding PC and Operating-System guides
- Documenting Mobile devices and Software
- Adding Networking and Router Information
- Finding and fixing broken or Outdated links

> Know something that belongs here? **[Help us document it](https://github.com/MRCYODev/CTT/issues)**.
